### Abstract
Short-horizon particulate matter nowcasting is critical for episodic air pollution alerts across industrial and agricultural corridors in Haryana. This study develops and evaluates an hourly $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ and $\text{PM}_{10}$ nowcasting system across **24 Haryana cities** trained on CPCB and HSPCB continuous monitoring records:

1. **Six-Model ML/DL Benchmark:** Evaluates classical machine learning regressors alongside four recurrent deep learning architectures (**LSTM, GRU, Bi-LSTM, and Bi-GRU**), isolating architecture-specific hyperparameters to ensure rigorous model-to-model comparability.
2. **Persistence Baseline & Statistical Testing:** Benchmarks all six architectures against a naive persistence baseline supported by formal statistical significance testing.
3. **Three-Pass Editorial & Methodological Audit:** Prepared in Springer Nature (`sn-jnl`) LaTeX format and audited through a structured three-pass peer-review pipeline (editorial desk-reject screening, methodology critique, and bibliographic verification).

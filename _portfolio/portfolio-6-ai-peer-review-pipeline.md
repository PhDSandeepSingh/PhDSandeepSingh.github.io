## System Overview
Designed and built two reusable AI-assisted academic research workflows to standardize methodological rigor, bibliographic verification, and journal compliance across Q1/Q2 submissions:

1. **Structured Three-Pass Manuscript Peer-Review Pipeline:**
   * **Pass 1 — Editorial Desk-Reject Screening:** Audits scope alignment, reporting checklists, ethics statements, and formatting compliance (`sn-jnl`, `svjour3`).
   * **Pass 2 — Methodological & Statistical Critique:** Screens for data leakage, improper cross-validation splits, missing baselines, and uncalibrated operating thresholds.
   * **Pass 3 — Point-by-Point Response & Revision Drafting:** Generates traceable reviewer response matrices and converts process-narrative drafts into formal scientific reporting register grounded in **COPE**, **ICMJE**, and **Springer Nature** guidelines.
2. **Scientific Writing & Report Authoring Tool:**
   * Streamlines LaTeX manuscript preparation, bibliography metadata verification, and reproducible audit table generation.

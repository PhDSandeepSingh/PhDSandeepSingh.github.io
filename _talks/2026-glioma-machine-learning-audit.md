This seminar examines recurring data leakage modes in computational biomarker studies, including pre-split oversampling, uncalibrated probability thresholds, and inflated performance metrics. Using our TCGA glioma study as a concrete case study, we demonstrate how strict nested cross-validation and pre-specified model locking yield realistic, clinically actionable diagnostic signals.
